The house behind the brand

New Bright is a brand of Al Rammah Factory.

Part of Al Rammah International Holding, a Saudi-owned group established in 1979 and headquartered in Al Khobar, Eastern Province.

Vision

To become a leading Saudi noodle brand in the region, recognized for distinctive flavor, dependable quality, practical convenience and continuous innovation.

Mission

To create high-quality Saudi-made noodles that make everyday meals simpler and more enjoyable through distinctive flavors, convenient preparation and the freedom to personalize every dish.
